# Fintrix Financial & Payments Knowledge Base

## Chargebacks
A chargeback is a formal transaction reversal initiated by the customer's issuing bank following a dispute regarding unauthorized debits, non-delivery of services, or fraudulent activity.

## Merchant Category Codes (MCC)
4-digit classification codes assigned by card networks/NPCI to describe the primary nature of a merchant's business operations.

## Risk Scoring
Fintrix calculates multi-factor risk combining dispute frequency, volume deviation, severity weights, and KYC verification compliance.
